Who we are

This site belongs to Rubixr Establishment (مؤسسة روبيكسآر), unified national number 7052361438, Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. Contact: rubixr.studio@gmail.com

The site itself collects nothing

The site has no accounts, stores nothing you type, sets no tracking cookies, and doesn't count or track visitors. Its fonts are hosted with the site, so browsing it sends no request to any other company.

The project name you type on the ticket stays in your browser. It only reaches us if you press “Send the ticket on WhatsApp” yourself.

When you contact us

  • What reaches us: only what you send us yourself on WhatsApp or by email, such as your name, your number or email address, your message, and your project's name if you send the ticket.
  • Why: to reply to you and discuss your project, and nothing else. We don't use it for any other purpose, sell it, or share it with anyone.
  • Is it required? No, contacting us is your choice. But without a way to reach you, we can't reply.
  • Where it's kept: in the studio's WhatsApp account and email inbox. WhatsApp is provided by Meta and email by Google. Their servers may be outside Saudi Arabia, and each has its own privacy policy.
  • When it's deleted: once it's no longer needed, meaning when the project ends or it's clear it won't go ahead. Sooner if you ask.

Your rights

Under Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law, you have the right to:

  • know what data we hold about you and why;
  • see it and get a copy;
  • ask us to correct, complete or update it;
  • ask us to delete it.

To use any of these rights, email us at rubixr.studio@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

Links to other services

The WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok and email links open services run by other companies, each with its own privacy policy.
