Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

What we collect

Nothing. The site has no accounts and no forms that we store, it sets no tracking cookies, and we don't count or track our visitors.

The ticket

The project name you type on the ticket stays in your browser. It only reaches us if you press “Send the ticket on WhatsApp” yourself, and then it arrives as an ordinary WhatsApp message.

Links to other services

  • The WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok and email links open services run by others, each with its own privacy policy.
  • The site's fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google receives your device's address when you open the page.
